Four contracts hold the design together

The choices of database, transport, and process boundaries can evolve. These architectural rules should remain stable.

01

Explicit inputs

Market facts, pricing estimates, parameters, account projections, and clocks arrive through named, versioned inputs.

02

One write boundary

Research, pricing, agents, and engines may propose. Only the gateway can send after account-aware authorization.

03

Replay is a product property

Startup state, ordered events, code, decisions, writes, and outcomes form one causal record.

04

Pricing and policy stay separate

A model estimates what an opportunity is worth. A strategy decides whether and how to act.

05

Account truth stays external

Engines use fast local projections. A dedicated position system owns the global, reconciled view.

06

Evals govern improvement

Agents propose changes. Fixed tests, unseen data, shadow runs, and bounded canaries decide what gets promoted.

02 · Runtime shape

Fast local state, no hidden truth

03 · Feeds and channels

Everything dynamic arrives as an event

Engines do not poll databases or call agents mid-decision. They consume declared channels. Each channel states its ordering, completeness, versioning, gap-recovery, and snapshot-to-live handoff guarantees.

Venue market channel

Books, trades, requests for quote, market status, lifecycle, and settlement events.

External data channel

Other venues, sports, crypto, economic data, weather, news, and contextual facts with provenance.

Order and fill channel

Acknowledgements, rejects, fills, corrections, busts, cancels, and venue reconciliation results.

Allocation and control channel

Capital budgets, limits, enablement, parameter versions, model versions, and governed operator intent.

Pricing channel

Fair values, uncertainty, feature lineage, freshness, and immutable model identity.

Timer channel

Recorded scheduled callbacks, deadlines, expiry, heartbeats, and session transitions without reading wall-clock time.

A common event envelope

Payloads differ, but the causal metadata should be stable enough for ordering, debugging, point-in-time reconstruction, and schema evolution.

04 · Reference database

The versioned snapshot defines the world

Reference data is not ambient lookup state. An engine begins from one immutable point-in-time snapshot that defines instruments, canonical events, mappings, fees, rules, and declared subscriptions.

Loaded before start

Reference snapshot

The snapshot binds exchange identifiers to durable domain identity. That makes replay stable even after a symbol, mapping, fee, or resolution rule changes.

  • Instrument and market definitions
  • Canonical event identity and relationships
  • Cross-venue and external-source mappings
  • Fees, tick sizes, lifecycle, and resolution rules
  • Declared feeds and engine subscription universe

Point-in-time contract

No invisible refresh

A running engine never discovers that its world changed through a background database read. A new reference version arrives as a control event or starts a new engine session. The selected version remains part of every episode.

  • Research can propose mappings; validation publishes them
  • Every artifact has effective time and provenance
  • Historical replay reloads the exact original version
  • Bad versions can be rejected or rolled back explicitly

06 · Position, risk, execution

Shared capital needs one account truth

An engine may know its attributed position. It cannot protect against another engine’s working orders, shared collateral, correlated event exposure, or an uncertain write. Those facts converge at the account boundary.

Engines propose

Trading intent

Desired action, price, size, strategy identity, decision lineage, expiry, and the assumptions that made the intent valid.

intent = decide( opportunity, estimate, local_state, parameters )
Account authority decides

Risk and reservations

The guard rechecks freshness, permissions, market state, self-trade, budget, collateral, working exposure, aggregate scenarios, and pending commitments. Approval creates a reservation before the write.

authorization = guard( intent, account_projection, limits, reservations )
Gateway sends

Idempotent mutation

The gateway owns venue credentials, rate limits, request identity, retry discipline, uncertain outcomes, reconciliation, and the lifecycle events that return to the ledger.

result = execute( authorized_intent, idempotency_key )

Safety boundary: pricing, research, scanners, strategies, and agents can propose actions. They cannot send them. Every outward action passes the account-aware pre-trade guard immediately before the write.

Four lifecycles, one contract

Ordinary orders, RFQ making, RFQ taking, and read-only measurement share normalized truth, deterministic decisions, account authorization, and episode capture.

Resting maker

Observe → quote → reconcile

Book event, complete opportunity, desired quote state, allocated budget, final guard, idempotent change, mark-out.

RFQ maker

Price → reserve → confirm

Request, fresh leg books, quote or pass, reserved loss, time-limited lifecycle, final recheck, attributed outcome.

RFQ taker

Probe → compare → accept

Candidate bundle, deterministic eligibility, reserved budget, private quotes, all-in comparison, accept or pass, probe score.

Shadow and replay

Rebuild → simulate → score

Point-in-time evidence, identical strategy, explicit fill model, observed outcome, candidate-versus-champion comparison.

07 · AI trader

Supervise the system, not the next packet

The AI trader takes the role of a portfolio-level human trader: it sees the whole book, finds failures and opportunities, proposes changes, and directs experiments. It stays out of the first version of the latency-sensitive path and never bypasses risk.

Fast · event driven

Engine loop

Receive event → update local projection → calculate decision → emit intent. No external query and no AI call.

Immediate · account aware

Risk and execution loop

Receive intent → check aggregate account → reserve exposure → send idempotent mutation → publish result.

Slow · supervisory

AI trader loop

Observe book and evals → propose models, parameters, allocations, hedges, or enablement → validate → publish a versioned control event.

08 · Eval definition

Profit is an outcome, not a diagnosis

An AI can only improve what the system can measure and explain. Complete episodes turn observed failures into reproducible tests. Fixed gates decide whether a proposed change survives outside the history that inspired it.

Definition

An eval is a repeatable test of a decision or system property.

It runs on a defined set of replayable episodes, uses decision-time information only, produces a score and diagnosis, and has a threshold for better, worse, or inconclusive.

Concrete question

Did this quote use a fresh, correctly calculated fair value?

Replay the request and each leg book as originally observed. Recompute with the production model. Check age, completeness, version, fees, and later mark-out. Attribute failure to data, pricing, or execution—not simply “bad trade.”

QuestionThe property we want to know
Episode setExactly which decisions are tested
TruthThe outcome or invariant used as evidence
ScorerHow evidence becomes a number
ThresholdPass, fail, or inconclusive
AttributionWhich component should change

09 · Pricing as a product

The model and strategy answer different questions

Keeping the contract explicit lets agents improve fair value without quietly changing trading policy—and improve policy without hiding a pricing error.

Pricing model

What is it worth?

Consumes market observations, reference data, external features, and a pinned model version. It does not know the desired profit and does not decide to trade.

price(inputs, model_version) → fair_value → uncertainty → as_of
Versioned estimate

A replayable contract

The result says what was believed, how certain it was, which evidence it used, and when that evidence was current.

{ fair: 0.347, uncertainty: 0.006, as_of: "decision time", model: "v17" }
Strategy

Should we act?

Combines the estimate with available prices, fees, risk budgets, inventory, and opportunity rules to produce no action or a trading intent.

decide(estimate, opportunity, fees, risk, parameters) → no_action | intent

Two replay-safe deployments: publish estimates as a versioned pricing feed, or run a pinned model component inside each engine. The feed favors sharing and independent scaling. The in-process component favors lower latency. Both must record exact inputs, output, model version, and timestamp.

The AI model-builder loop

A pricing candidate earns publication through calibration and downstream decision value across unseen periods—not because one backtest became more profitable.

1 · HypothesizeExplain an errorName a calibration failure or missing relationship

12 · Unit of learning

The complete trade episode

A feed tells us what happened. An episode adds the causal spine needed to answer why: inputs, versions, decision, authorization, venue response, and later outcome joined under one identity.

1 · OpportunityWhat arrived?Trigger, raw references, normalized event, source and receive times
2 · ContextWhat was known?Books, reference version, prices, parameters, positions, code
3 · DecisionWhy this action?Fair value, uncertainty, alternatives, rejection reasons, price, size
4 · AuthorizationWas it allowed?Account risk, reservations, permissions, idempotency
5 · ExecutionWhat happened?Acks, fills, rejects, cancels, latency, attribution
6 · AfterlifeWas it good?Mark-outs, hedge cost, settlement, realized profit, capital duration

13 · Anti-overfit promotion

Design against gaming the score

An agent will exploit any metric—including flaws in the simulator. These are architectural constraints, not research etiquette.

Freeze the eval first

Define slices, scores, thresholds, and exclusions before the candidate. A rejection cannot rewrite its test.

Seal temporal holdouts

Agents inspect development periods. Final evaluation uses later periods they have never seen.

Use decision-time inputs

Later outcomes score a decision. They never leak into what the decision could know.

Label modeled truth

Decision replay can be exact. Counterfactual fills are simulated and must include sensitivity analysis.

Require robustness

Nearby parameters, other periods, market families, and plausible costs should preserve the result.

Charge for search

Track every candidate. Confidence falls when many variants mine the same history for one winner.

Test scale and overlap

Include size, fees, impact, shared legs, concentration, and capital duration—not five profitable dollars.

Separate maker and judge

No agent both proposes a change and controls the sealed evidence or promotion decision.

The promotion ladder

Every stage can reject. Live stages have bounded authority, preset rollback conditions, and a durable return path to the current champion.

1RegressionSafety, correctness, replay, invariants
2Development replayDiagnose and iterate on visible history
3Sealed holdoutUnseen temporal evidence and fixed scores
4Live shadowCurrent flow with no venue writes
5Bounded canarySmall exposure, guardrails, auto rollback
6ProductionVersioned champion with monitored rollback

14 · Agent organization

A trading desk made of bounded roles

Different agents optimize different eval families. Research roles do not receive execution authority, and an independent role challenges each candidate.

Research desk

Strategy investigator

Finds opportunity and failure clusters, proposes hypotheses, and predicts score movement before implementation.

May propose · cannot promote or trade
Pricing desk

Model and data agent

Improves mappings, features, calibration, dependency estimates, and uncertainty measurement.

May build · cannot alter holdouts
Execution desk

Performance agent

Profiles source-to-ack latency and improves throughput while preserving replay and economic behavior.

May optimize · must preserve parity
Portfolio desk

AI trader

Prioritizes experiments, allocates capital, proposes enablement and hedges, and weighs competing metrics.

May propose controls · risk remains binding
Independent judge

Evaluator and red team

Runs sealed suites, challenges attribution, searches for leakage and simulator exploits, and can veto.

May veto · cannot modify candidate
Control plane

Release steward

Verifies evidence, stages shadow and canary runs, watches rollback metrics, and preserves the champion.

Only governed workflows may promote

Every proposal must be falsifiable

The contract names one change, its expected effect, the slice that should move, what must not regress, and what evidence can support promotion.

candidate:
  change: refresh every leg at decision time
  target_eval: pricing.fresh_book_markout
  expected: fewer false-cheap classifications
  primary_slice: stale_book_age
  must_not_regress:
    - decision_latency_p99
    - missing_leg_rate
    - deterministic_replay

The hypothesis predicts a narrow, attributable effect and declares its protected metrics before implementation.

decision:
  result: promote | reject | inconclusive
  evidence:
    development: visible
    holdout: sealed until scoring
    shadow: current live inputs
  uncertainty: confidence interval
  known_limits: fill counterfactual is modeled
  rollback_to: previous version
  lineage: code + config + data + scorer

The result keeps its evidence and lineage so a later evaluator can reproduce the claim instead of trusting a summary.
