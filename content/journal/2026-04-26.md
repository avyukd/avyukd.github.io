## Praxis

Got the raw and analysis pipelines up and running on [[praxis]] today. Trying to get the full v3 shipped by tonight.

Praxis is an AI-managed research system I'm building for trading. The core insight driving it: my problem as a trader isn't that I lack conviction — it's that I can't pull the trigger big on positions even when I believe in them. The fix isn't to become smarter. It's to be faster. Move quicker than the long-term guys, but with serious AI-driven research underpinning every decision rather than gut feel.

The specific edge I'm going after: nobody is paying attention to sub-$10M [[microcaps]]. There are hundreds of them, and any serious human analyst would take hours to properly evaluate one. Praxis can do a full deep dive in under five minutes. That's not just a productivity gain — it's a structural arbitrage. A long-term investor who knows a stock well still has to sit down and piece together the thesis. By the time they do, I've already been through the whole universe.
